about saving jars

Visualise your savings growing and keep motivated. Choose your theme (a jar of coins, a fuel gauge, or a street of houses that light up one by one), pick a colour palette that feels like yours, then log each saving and watch it grow towards your goal. It's a gentle, no-pressure tracker: every amount you set aside, however small, takes you closer, and a slow week won't undo the progress you've already made. Everything stays private in your own browser.

how to use it
  1. On the Home tab, name your goal and set a target amount. Add a target date and a starting balance if you like.
  2. Choose how you want to see progress: the jar, the fuel gauge or the house.
  3. Log a saving whenever you put money aside, from Home or the Log tab. You can pick a past date.
  4. Watch your picture fill up as you pass each level, and set small rewards for each milestone in the Journal tab.
  5. Tick your weekly habit tracker: saved something, felt tempted, stayed on track.
  6. Once a week, write a short reflection in the Journal tab. Your history is kept in last 7 days and monthly groups.
  7. Reached your goal? Choose start new challenge in Settings to keep its history and begin the next one. Export a backup now and then.

privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Saving Jars is a single, self-contained app that runs in your browser. This page explains what data exists, where it lives, and who (if anyone) sees it.

the short version

Your saving data never leaves your device. Everything you log — goals, savings, habit tracker, journal entries — is stored only in your own browser's local storage. It isn't sent to me, to a server, or to any third party. I have no way to see it.

what's stored, and where

When you use Saving Jars, the app saves your goals, logged savings, chosen palette, habit tracker entries and journal reflections in your browser's localStorage — a storage area built into your browser, tied to your device only.

  • It is not uploaded anywhere automatically.
  • It stays put if you close the tab or restart your device, but is lost if you clear your browser's site data, use a different browser or device, or use a private/incognito window.
  • You can export a backup file from Settings at any time, and re-import it later or on another device.

buying saving jars

Payment is handled by a third-party checkout provider, who acts as the merchant of record for the sale. They process your payment details and email address in order to complete the purchase, issue a receipt, and deliver the file to you — I don't see or store your card details at any point. Their own privacy policy governs how they handle that information.

the feedback form

If you choose to fill in the in-app feedback form, it's provided by a third-party form service (Tally), and whatever you type is sent to them and passed on to me. It's entirely optional — the app works fully without ever opening it.

joining the mailing list

If you sign up for updates or a free download via the email form on this site, your email address is stored by our email provider (Kit) so that we can send you those emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email you receive.

cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is the app's own localStorage data described above, which exists to make the app work for you, not to track you.

your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to ask what personal data (if any) is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it's used. Because saving data lives only on your own device, most of this simply means: it's already entirely in your control, and you can delete it yourself at any time by clearing your browser data or using the in-app reset option.

For anything relating to a purchase, the checkout provider's own privacy policy and support channels apply directly.

changes to this policy

If anything here changes — a new provider, a new feature that stores data differently — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to: future.sanctuary00@gmail.com.

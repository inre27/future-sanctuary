how it works

four steps to watching it grow

A simple visual way to watch your savings grow and keep motivated.

01
set a theme and colour

Pick your visual — a jar, a fuel gauge or a street of houses — and choose from four earthy colour palettes: Potting Shed, Lido Breeze, Terracotta Picnic or Mustard Moon. Change either any time.

02
set a goal

Name what you're saving for, your target amount, and (if you like) a date.

03
log what you put aside

Add a saving whenever you have one — a fiver, a leftover coffee budget, a whole payday. Takes seconds, and slips don't undo your progress.

04
watch it grow

Your jar, gauge or houses fill level by level as you get closer, with a gentle message at each milestone.

faq

questions

Is this a subscription? +
No. Saving Jars is a one-time purchase. Pay once and it's yours indefinitely — no recurring charges, ever.
Do I need an account or to link a bank? +
No. There's no login and no bank connection. You log what you've saved yourself, whenever you like.
Where is my data, and can I back it up? +
Everything is stored in your browser, on your own device. You can export a backup at any time from Settings and re-import it if you switch devices or clear your browser data.
Does it work on my phone, and offline? +
Yes to both. The layout adjusts for smaller screens, and once the page has loaded it works without an internet connection.
Can I use it for more than one goal? +
Yes — start a new challenge whenever you finish one or want to change direction. Your past challenges are archived and stay easy to look back on.
What about refunds and updates? +
There's a 14-day refund promise if it's not for you. Future updates to the app are included at no extra cost.

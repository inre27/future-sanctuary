The 6 Spending Triggers Workbook

A diagnostic guide for noticing the feeling underneath an unplanned purchase — before the urge becomes a checkout.

Most impulse purchases are set off by one of these six emotional states. They're not character flaws — they're patterns. Which ones do you recognise in yourself?

stress
I need relief.
Tension, pressure, or overwhelm.
Ask: what am I trying to make easier right now?
boredom
I need stimulation.
Restlessness, scrolling, low energy.
Ask: what would give me novelty without spending?
sadness
I need comfort.
Heaviness, loneliness, or disappointment.
Ask: what would actually soothe me?
social pressure
I need belonging.
Comparison, FOMO, or the urge to keep up.
Ask: who am I trying to feel closer to?
reward
I need recognition.
Feeling like you’ve earned something.
Ask: how can I celebrate without making this a purchase?
control
I need agency.
Uncertainty, chaos, or a need to fix things.
Ask: what small action would give me control?
How to use this page: when an urge appears, don’t ask “what should I buy?” first. Ask which trigger is present, then move to page two and decode the feeling underneath it.

questions

things people ask first

Is Bloom a subscription?

No. It is a single one-off payment of £3.49. There is nothing to renew, and future updates to this version are included.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything you log lives in your own browser on your own device, and I cannot see it. Because of that, back it up now and then using the export option in Settings. The privacy policy has the detail.

Do I need an account or to log in?

No account, no login and no app store. You open the file in your browser and start your first challenge.

What happens if I spend on a day?

You log it as spent and carry on. Your garden does not wipe or reset; a spent day just does not add growth. Resisted days count too, so a day you wanted to buy and didn\u2019t still helps the garden grow.

Do I have to enter how much I spend?

No. Bloom is about days, not amounts. You choose what you are resisting from ten categories and mark each day, and it never asks for bank details.

What if it is not for me?

There is a 14-day refund promise, no questions asked. See the refund terms.

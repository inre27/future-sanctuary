how it works

three small steps, no spreadsheet

Pause is built around the moment of the urge, not around a budget. It takes a minute or two a day.

1

Check in once a day: no spend, or spent. Or log the specific thing you nearly bought, and whether you resisted it.

questions

things people ask

Does Pause connect to my bank?

No. There's no bank link and no login. You log things yourself, which is part of what makes it work: the act of noticing is the point.

Is it a subscription?

No. Free Pause costs nothing, and full Pause is a single £4.99 payment. There's nothing to renew.

What's the difference between free and full?

Free Pause gives you the dashboard, daily log, decoder, levels and streaks in the dune and dusted theme. Full Pause adds the weekly reflection with an if-then plan, insights, and three more colour themes.

Where is my data, and what if I clear my browser?

Everything you log is stored on your own device, in your browser. It isn't sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data, switching browser or device, or using a private window can lose it, so use the export button in the app to keep a backup. More in the privacy policy.

I've bought full Pause. How do I unlock it?

Your licence key arrives by email after purchase. Open Pause, go to Settings, paste the key into the licence section and tap activate. Everything switches on in place, with the same data, nothing to move over.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Pause runs in your browser and is designed to work on a phone as well as a laptop. Open it, and you can add it to your home screen if you like.

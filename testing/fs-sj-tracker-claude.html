How to use this tracker

Fill it in browser (every field is interactive and your progress bar updates live), or print it and fill it in by hand. Start with Exercise 1 — your target amount feeds the progress bar and milestone amounts automatically, so it's worth setting that first. Come back to the monthly log and momentum tracker regularly as you save.

Your answers save automatically

If you're working in the browser version, your progress saves to this browser as you go — no need to press anything. It's tied to this device though, so if you switch devices or browsers, use the Save / Export button at the bottom of the page to download a JSON backup, then Import Answers to bring it back in later.

Exercise 03
Your Milestone Map

Big goals are easier to sustain when they're broken into smaller wins along the way. These four milestones are calculated automatically from your target amount — give each one a small, low-cost reward so reaching it actually feels like something.

Why milestones work: a single distant target can feel abstract and easy to deprioritise. Four visible checkpoints — each with its own small reward — give your brain regular proof that the plan is working, long before the big goal itself is met.

Exercise 04 · Monthly Savings Log
Exercise 04
Monthly Savings Log

A simple month-by-month record. It doesn't need to be a fixed amount every month — the point is to see the pattern of what actually went in, not to judge any single month against another.

Month Amount saved Notes

Consistency matters more than amount. A £20 month is real data, not a failure — it tells you something about what was going on that month. Fill in every row you can, even the low ones, rather than skipping months that felt disappointing.

Exercise 06 · Reflection & What's Next
Exercise 06
Quarterly Reflection

Every few months, come back to these questions. Honest reflection — not judgement — is what turns a savings goal into a habit that actually sticks.

1. Am I roughly on track for my target date?
2. What made saving easiest this quarter?
3. What made it hardest?
4. Does my target or timeframe need adjusting?

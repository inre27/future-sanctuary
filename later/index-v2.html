the idea behind it

most tools for modern life
are built to hold your attention.
these are built to give it back.

Future Sanctuary is an umbrella for a small, growing family of apps built around the same conviction: the parts of life that feel automatic — spending, focus, decisions, work — get easier once you can actually see the pattern underneath them. Each app is narrow on purpose. Understand one thing well, rather than manage everything badly.

🌿
calm by design

No streak-shaming, no red badges, no dark patterns designed to keep you opening the app. If a tool isn't useful anymore, you should be able to just stop.

🔍
pattern over performance

Every app starts from the same question: not "how do I do better?" but "what's actually happening, and why?" Awareness first. Change tends to follow.

🗝
yours, not ours

No accounts unless there's a real reason for one. Your data lives in your browser by default, and you can always export it and take it with you.

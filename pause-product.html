how it works

three steps to understanding your spending

Pause isn't a budgeting app. It's a self-awareness tool — designed to help you understand the patterns behind your impulse spending, not just record the numbers.

01
log your day

Check in daily — takes 30 seconds. Log whether you spent, how difficult it was to resist, and optionally any specific items you bought or considered buying.

02
decode each impulse

Tap any logged item to decode it. What triggered it? What were you really looking for — relief, escape, FOMO, reward? What did you do instead?

03
get your plan

Pause analyses your decoded impulses and generates a personalised if-then plan — a single, specific sentence for your exact trigger pattern. Then reflect weekly and watch your patterns shift.

faq

questions

Do I need to download anything? +
No. Pause runs entirely in your browser — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge. Just open the link and start. Nothing to install.
Will my data be saved between sessions? +
Yes — your data is stored in your browser's local storage and persists between sessions automatically. You can also export a JSON backup at any time and re-import it if needed.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes. Pause is designed to work on both desktop and mobile browsers. The layout adjusts for smaller screens.
Is this a subscription? +
No. This is a one-time purchase. You pay once and have access to the current version of Pause indefinitely. No recurring charges, ever.
What if I clear my browser data? +
Export a JSON backup first using the Export button in Settings. You can re-import it at any time to restore all your data. Set up the weekly reminder in Settings and you'll never forget.
What is the impulse decoder? +
The Impulse Decoder is a popup that appears when you tap "decode" on any logged item. It lets you tag the trigger (what set off the urge) and the core driver (the underlying emotional need) for that specific purchase. Over time, your decoded items build a pattern map in the Insights panel.
